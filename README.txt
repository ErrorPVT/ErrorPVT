ErrorPVT website starter
==========================

Files:
- index.html: responsive homepage and membership sections
- terms.html: terms template (must be reviewed and customized)
- privacy.html: privacy template (must be completed before launch)

Before publishing:
1. Replace ADD_YOUR_TRAINER_WHOP_LINK and ADD_YOUR_ELITE_WHOP_LINK in index.html with your live Whop product URLs.
2. Update prices, features, billing and cancellation language to match your actual products.
3. Configure support@errorpvt.com with your email provider, or replace it with a real support address.
4. Complete and review Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. These are templates, not legal advice.
5. Upload the files to your hosting provider and point errorpvt.com to that host.
6. Test the site on mobile and desktop and verify all checkout links.

This is a static starter site. It does not itself process payments or manage Discord roles; use Whop's checkout and properly configured Discord integration for those functions.
